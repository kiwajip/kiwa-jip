ABOUT THE CRAFT

Based in Frankfurt am Main, KIWA specializes in custom ornamental and geometric tattoos. Bridging traditional Korean motifs—such as Dancheong, clouds, and snow—with contemporary linework and organic body flow to create timeless wearable art.

BOOKING GUIDE & FAQ

Q. How does custom design work and what about the deposit?

Custom designs include a deposit requirement of €100 to secure your date. This deposit is fully included in the total tattoo price, so you do not need to pay it twice.

Q. How should I prepare for my session?

Get a good night's sleep, eat well before your session, and drink plenty of water. Avoid alcohol and blood thinners 24 hours prior to your appointment.
