Healing Guide

Tattoo Aftercare

Keep the initial protective film or bandage on for the duration recommended by KIWA (usually 24 to 48 hours). Fluid buildup underneath is normal. If the bandage tears or leaks, gently remove it early, wash the area with lukewarm water and mild, fragrance-free soap, pat dry with a clean paper towel, and let it breathe.
Wash your tattoo 2 times a day with clean hands, mild soap, and warm water. Always pat dry—never rub. Apply a very thin layer of recommended aftercare ointment or fragrance-free lotion. Do not oversaturate; your skin needs to breathe. Avoid petroleum-based products unless specifically advised.
To ensure your lines and dotwork stay crisp and infection-free, strictly avoid the following for at least 2–3 weeks:
  • No swimming pools, saunas, baths, or direct soaking in water.
  • No direct sunlight or tanning beds on the fresh tattoo.
  • Do not pick, scratch, or peel scabs or flaking skin.
  • Avoid tight or abrasive clothing rubbing constantly against the tattoo.
