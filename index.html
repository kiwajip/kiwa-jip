Q. How does custom design work and what about the deposit?
Custom designs include a deposit requirement of €100 to secure your date. This deposit is fully included in the total tattoo price, so you do not need to pay it twice.
Q. How should I prepare for my session?
Get a good night's sleep, eat a solid meal before coming, and stay well hydrated. Avoid alcohol and blood thinners 24 hours prior to your appointment.
1. Film / Bandage Removal
If using protective film, leave it on for 3 to 4 days. If fluid pools excessively, you can remove it earlier. Gently wash the area with lukewarm water and mild soap.
2. Moisturizing
Apply a thin layer of specialized tattoo aftercare ointment or fragrance-free lotion 2 to 3 times a day. Do not over-apply; the skin needs to breathe.
3. Important Rules
Do not pick, scratch, or peel the scabs/skin as it heals. Avoid swimming pools, saunas, direct sunlight, and intense workouts for at least 2 weeks.
