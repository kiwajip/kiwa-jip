Booking Guide & FAQ

Q. How does custom design work and what about the deposit? +
Custom designs include a deposit requirement of €100 to secure your date. This deposit is fully included in the total tattoo price.
Q. How should I prepare for my session? +
Get a good night's sleep, eat a solid meal, and stay well hydrated. Avoid alcohol and blood thinners 24 hours prior.

Tattoo Aftercare Guide

1. Film / Bandage Removal +
Leave protective film on for 3 to 4 days. Gently wash the area with lukewarm water and mild soap after removal.
2. Moisturizing +
Apply a thin layer of specialized tattoo ointment or fragrance-free lotion 2 to 3 times a day.
3. Important Rules (Don'ts) +
Do not pick scabs. Avoid pools, saunas, direct sun, and intense workouts for at least 2 weeks.
